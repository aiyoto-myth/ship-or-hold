# Ship or Hold

Free weather check for people who mail live plants and animals: paste the week's orders and each one gets the National Weather Service forecast where you ship from, at the carrier hub and at the buyer's door, with a call: ship, heat pack, insulated box, cold pack, or hold, plus a note for the buyer.

Live: https://samsbench.com/ship-or-hold/

ZIP locations: GeoNames postal codes (CC BY 4.0, https://www.geonames.org/) and US Census 2024 ZCTA centers (public domain). Forecasts: api.weather.gov (US government, public domain).
