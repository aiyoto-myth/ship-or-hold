Know another seller who mails live plants or animals? Send them the page.

Forecasts come from the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), fetched when you press Check. Forecasts change: check again the morning you pack. US addresses only. ZIP locations: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and US Census ZIP-area centers.

The temperature bands are the published guidelines named under the scale (USARK, Reptiles Express, Palmstreet, Predatory Plants). Set your own if you know your stock better. This page makes the call from the numbers; the decision to ship is yours. Your orders and settings stay on this device; only ZIP-area coordinates go to the weather service, and only the email you type to unlock Pro is sent, to check the purchase.
